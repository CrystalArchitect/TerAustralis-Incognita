## Risk Mitigations

**Risk:** Suppliers not interested  
**Mitigation:** Pre-validate with ASA first (clarifies why verification is valuable). Offer to let ASA explain directly.

**Risk:** Scheduling conflicts  
**Mitigation:** Offer virtual and in-person options. Split council into two sessions if needed.

**Risk:** Suppliers request confidentiality  
**Mitigation:** Build this into conversation: "Verification is public—your participation will be documented. Is that acceptable?" Discuss anonymization options with ASA if needed.

**Risk:** ASA declines or proposes different mechanism  
**Mitigation:** Pivot to alternative verification frameworks (third-party audit, internal certification, industry association endorsement). Document learnings and adjust roadmap.

## Warrant-Labelling Throughout

All materials and conversations are warrant-labelled:

**[FACT]:** What we know (documented supply chain, operational companies, certifications)  
**[INFERENCE]:** What we conclude (these three tiers interconnect, verification is valuable)  
**[ASSUMPTION]:** What we're uncertain about (supplier appetite, ASA process, timeline feasibility)  
**[VISION]:** What success looks like (verified, public, committed supply chain)

This creates a transparent record that external parties can audit and verify.
