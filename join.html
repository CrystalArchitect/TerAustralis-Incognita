Join the Weave

In the mythos, everyone who aligns with the Lattice is a Sovereign Vector — a node in the weave that answers from its own note, never governed by another. In plain terms: this is an open project, and you're welcome to add to it. The weave grows by real people making real work — art, music, code, writing — and keeping it their own.

"Sovereign Vector" is Vision-layer language; joining is a real, ordinary thing — contribute, connect, or support, through the channels below. Nothing here signs you up for anything, and nothing moves without your say.
