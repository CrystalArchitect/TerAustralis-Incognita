### Task 2: Prepare Agenda & Materials

**Joint Council Call Agenda (2-3 hours total)**

**Opening (15 minutes)**
- Welcome, context-setting, why this conversation matters
- "This is a conversation about proving Australian sovereignty works. Each of you is critical. Lynas says 'we can extract and process REE.' Magellan says 'we can manufacture aerospace components from those materials.' ELA says 'we can integrate and launch those components.' Together, that's the proof."
- Ground rules: confidentiality of each company's individual constraints, transparency of capability claims, focus on what's possible, not what's impossible

**Mutual Context (20 minutes)**
- Each supplier gives 5-minute overview of their role and current operations
  - Lynas: Mount Weld extraction, Perth processing, current aerospace supply relationships
  - Magellan: Manufacturing sites, aerospace certifications, current cross-tier supplier relationships
  - ELA: Launch facilities, current payload integration experience, licensing
- Other suppliers listen and ask clarifying questions (goal: understand each other's actual capabilities)

**Supply Chain Coordination (30 minutes)**
- Map the three pathways explicitly:
  - **Path 1 (REE→avionics):** Lynas Mount Weld → Lynas Perth → Magellan [which facility?] → ELA [which launch provider? timeline?]
  - **Path 2 (Titanium→structure):** Iluka → Magellan → Southern Launch [how does this differ from Path 1?]
  - **Path 3 (Lithium→power):** Pilbara → Magellan → Gilmour Space [how does this differ?]
- For each path, identify: transaction points, specification handoffs, certification requirements, timeline constraints
- Goal: "Can we describe each pathway so clearly that an independent auditor could verify it exists?"

**ASA Verification Roadmap (30 minutes)**
- Explain ASA Statement of Engagement scope and process (reference the Phase 1 ASA engagement brief)
- Concrete timeline:
  - Week 11-12 (Oct 21-Nov 4): Drafting ASA SoE proposal with supplier input; securing written statements of capability
  - Late November: ASA lodges formal engagement letter
  - Q4 2026-Q1 2027: ASA verification audit (timeline to be confirmed with ASA)
- For each supplier: "What documentation will ASA need from you? Do you have it? What's the timeline to provide it?"
- Identify any regulatory or confidentiality blockers upfront

**Coordination Discussion & Commitments (45 minutes)**
- "If we move forward, here's what each of you is committing to: [supplier-specific commitment]"
- Lynas: Written statement of Mount Weld extraction and Perth processing capability; willingness to participate in ASA facility review
- Magellan: Written statement of manufacturing capability and cross-tier supply relationships; documentation of supplier agreements or certifications (redacted as needed)
- ELA: Written statement of launch integration capability and operational readiness; documentation of licensing and facility readiness
- Group discussion: "Are these commitments realistic? What needs to change?"
- Document any conditional commitments (e.g., "Yes, if ASA agrees to [X]")

**Next Steps & Accountability (15 minutes)**
- "By [date], we'll follow up individually with each of you to secure written statements."
- "By [date], we'll draft the ASA SoE proposal incorporating your feedback."
- "By [date], we'll lodge the engagement letter with ASA."
- Confirm who is the decision-maker for each company going forward (for phase 4 follow-ups)
- Agree on communication cadence (weekly email update? monthly calls?)

**Close (5 minutes)**
- Thank everyone
- Reinforce the vision: "This is how Australian sovereignty gets verified. Three suppliers, transparent about what they can do, working together. That's the proof."
