# ASA Pre-engagement Brief
## Australian Standards Association Statement of Engagement Verification

**Purpose:** Pre-validate Small Council scope with ASA before approaching suppliers  
**Status:** Ready for ASA contact (weeks 7-9)  
**Date prepared:** 2026-09-03

---

## Context

TerAustralis Incognita is preparing to lodge a Statement of Engagement (SoE) with the Australian Standards Association to verify a complete sovereign supply chain for space-capable components.

**The Claim:** An Australian satellite or launch vehicle component can be fully sourced, processed, manufactured, and integrated within Australian facilities using domestically mined critical minerals and ASA-verified manufacturing processes.

**The Participants:** Three lead Australian suppliers have been identified:
- **Lynas Rare Earths** (Tier 1-2: critical minerals extraction + processing)
- **Magellan Aerospace** (Tier 3: aerospace component manufacturing, AS 9100 certified)
- **Equatorial Launch Australia** (Tier 4: launch integration)

---

## Questions for ASA

### Process Questions
1. **Is Statement of Engagement the appropriate mechanism** for verifying a multi-tier industrial supply chain?
   - Alternative mechanisms: third-party audit, industry association endorsement, internal certification?

2. **What are the SoE requirements?**
   - Documentation scope (what evidence is required)?
   - Verification depth (factory audits, documentation review, certification check)?
   - Timeline (how long does an SoE typically take from lodgement to completion)?

3. **What is the SoE process?**
   - Who at ASA handles SoE lodgements?
   - What is the formal process (application, review, site visits, report)?
   - What standards will be applied (AS 9100, ISO 9001, mining industry standards, etc.)?

4. **What does SoE cost?**
   - Application fee?
   - Audit/verification costs?
   - Ongoing registration costs?

5. **Can an SoE cover multiple suppliers and multiple pathways?**
   - We have 3 suppliers participating in different combinations of 3 exemplar pathways
   - Can the SoE be one integrated verification or do we need separate SoE for each pathway?

### Scope Questions

6. **What would the verification scope include?**
   - Capability verification (can the suppliers do what they claim)?
   - Relationship verification (do the suppliers actually transact with each other, or just theoretically could)?
   - Standards compliance (are their existing certifications valid, or does SoE add new verification)?

7. **What about supply chain coordination?**
   - Lynas → Magellan → ELA (forward integration)
   - Does SoE require us to prove these relationships exist, or just that each tier meets standards independently?

8. **Is confidentiality available?**
   - Can supplier identities be anonymized (e.g., "Tier 1 REE processor") or must they be named?
   - Are supplier relationships confidential or public?

### Timeline Questions

9. **What is a realistic timeline?**
   - Lodgement to first audit: weeks?
   - Audit to report: weeks?
   - Total SoE completion: typical duration?
   - **Our constraint:** We want to lodge before end of 2026 (week 12 of 90-day roadmap)

10. **What are typical SoE outcomes?**
    - SoE approved / verified?
    - SoE pending / in progress?
    - SoE conditional (pending supplier actions)?

## What We're Asking ASA For

1. **Guidance on SoE appropriateness** — Is this the right mechanism?
2. **Process clarification** — Steps, timeline, cost
3. **Scope validation** — Do these three pathways make sense as a single SoE or three separate ones?
4. **Supplier confidentiality** — Can we keep it confidential or must suppliers be named?
5. **Relationship verification** — Must we prove inter-tier transactions or just tier-by-tier standards compliance?
6. **Next steps** — If ASA is interested, what's the formal lodgement process?

## Talking Points for ASA Contact

**Opening:** "TerAustralis Incognita is documenting a sovereign Australian supply chain for space-capable components. We've identified three lead suppliers and want to verify the chain through formal ASA engagement. We're calling to understand whether Statement of Engagement is the right mechanism, and to clarify scope and process before we approach the suppliers."

**Key message:** "This is a first-of-kind verification effort. We want it to be rigorous, transparent, and ASA-endorsed so it carries real credibility. We're asking for guidance on how ASA can best help."

**Close:** "Can we schedule a brief call with the relevant SoE coordinator to discuss scope, process, and timeline? We're working toward a lodgement before end of 2026."
