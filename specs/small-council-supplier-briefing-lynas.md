## The Opportunity

Lynas Rare Earths is invited to participate in formal verification of Australia's sovereign supply chain for space-capable components. This verification will be conducted through an Australian Standards Association (ASA) Statement of Engagement and will publicly recognize Lynas as the critical foundation of Australian aerospace independence.

---

## What Lynas Is Part Of

**Pathway: Rare Earth Elements → Avionics**

```
Mount Weld Rare Earth Mine (Western Australia)
    ↓ [Lynas extracts REE ore]
Lynas Processing Plant (Perth, Western Australia)
    ↓ [Lynas purifies to MREO, separates rare earth elements]
Magellan Aerospace (Victoria)
    ↓ [Magellan manufactures avionics components using REE]
Equatorial Launch Australia (WA/NT)
    ↓ [ELA integrates and launches]
Space-ready satellite/launch vehicle component
```

**Your Role:** Tier 1-2 (critical minerals extraction + processing)
- You are the **only Australian source** for REE processing at scale
- Mount Weld is world-leading in REE reserves
- Your Perth processing facility completes the domestic chain
- Without Lynas, this pathway cannot be Australian-sovereign

---

## What We're Asking

Participate in formal verification that confirms:
1. **Operational capability:** Mount Weld and Perth processing are operational and certified
2. **Standards compliance:** Your facilities meet ISO 9001 and industry-specific standards
3. **Supply chain participation:** You are willing to supply processed REE to Magellan Aerospace for space component manufacturing

**You are not being asked to:**
- Change your operations
- Modify your manufacturing processes
- Accept new compliance burden
- Keep suppliers secret

**You are being asked to:**
- Confirm publicly that you participate in this supply chain
- Allow ASA verification of your existing certifications
- Participate in one joint stakeholder call with Magellan and Equatorial Launch Australia
- Commit to exploring formal ASA Statement of Engagement

## The Process

**ASA Statement of Engagement** is a formal verification mechanism where ASA:
1. Evaluates supplier capabilities and standards compliance
2. Audits documentation and (if needed) facility operations
3. Issues a verification report confirming the supply chain is real and standards-aligned
4. This report becomes public-facing proof of sovereignty

It is **not** a new certification or compliance burden—ASA reviews your existing certifications and operational record.

We are assuming Lynas sees **strategic value** in being publicly named as Australia's REE anchor for space sovereignty. We're not sure yet if this aligns with your investor story, regulatory positioning, or competitive strategy.

## Questions We Expect You'll Have

**Q: What's the cost to Lynas?**  
A: No direct cost. ASA fees are covered by TerAustralis. You may incur internal coordination costs (probably <20 hours of staff time total for documentation and audit participation).

**Q: Will this be public?**  
A: Yes. The SoE will name Lynas as a participant, and the verification report will be public. This is by design—sovereignty verification only matters if it's externally verifiable.

**Q: What if we want confidentiality?**  
A: The SoE process requires transparency. However, we can discuss with ASA whether specific cost/margin details can be anonymized while confirming operational participation. This is a negotiation point.

**Q: How much time does this take?**  
A: For you, approximately:
- Individual conversation with TerAustralis: 30-45 minutes (week 10)
- Small Council call with three suppliers: 2-3 hours (week 10-11)
- Follow-up calls with TerAustralis and ASA: 2-4 hours total (weeks 11-12)
- Documentation/audit support: 8-12 hours (weeks 11-12 and into Q1)
- **Total: ~15-25 hours of staff time over 3 months**

**Q: Can we consult our board before committing?**  
A: Absolutely. We expect you'll need internal approval. Our timeline allows for that—individual conversation in week 10 is exploratory; formal commitment would follow the Small Council call in week 11-12.

**Q: Who else will be at the Small Council call?**  
A: Magellan Aerospace (manufacturing) and Equatorial Launch Australia (launch integration). Both have been identified as critical participants in the REE→avionics pathway. They'll be exploring the same opportunity you are.
