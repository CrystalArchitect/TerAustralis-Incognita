Executive Summary

Australia possesses a complete sovereign industrial chain for space-capable components: from critical mineral extraction through advanced processing, aerospace-grade manufacturing, and launch integration. This document demonstrates three exemplar pathways, identifies lead Australian suppliers, and outlines the ASA engagement process for standards-aligned verification.

ASA Statement of Engagement (SoE)

The Australian Standards Association (ASA) is Australia's peak standards body. An SoE formally declares that an organisation is adopting Australian Standards, with verifiable practices and independent audit.

Lodgement Process

  1. Notify ASA of intention to lodge SoE for integrated chain
  2. Identify standards: AS 9100 (aerospace quality), AS/NZS ISO 17025 (testing), ISO 9001 (quality)
  3. Third-party audit: Commission independent auditor to verify compliance
  4. Submit SoE to ASA Registry: Public listing of verified capability
  5. Publish verification: Externally verifiable by any third party

Outcome: An Australian entity can demonstrably claim "100% Australian sourced and manufactured space component" with formal ASA-backed verification, verifiable by outsiders.
