## The Opportunity

Magellan Aerospace is invited to participate in formal verification of Australia's sovereign supply chain for space-capable components. This verification will be conducted through an Australian Standards Association (ASA) Statement of Engagement and will publicly recognize Magellan as the **critical manufacturing hub** connecting Australian critical minerals to space-ready aerospace components.

---

## What Magellan Is Part Of

**Magellan appears in ALL THREE exemplar pathways:**

```
Pathway 1: Rare Earth → Avionics
  Lynas processing (Perth) → [MAGELLAN] → Equatorial Launch
  
Pathway 2: Titanium → Structural Components
  Iluka processing (WA) → [MAGELLAN] → Southern Launch
  
Pathway 3: Lithium → Power Systems
  Pilbara processing (WA) → [MAGELLAN or RUAG Space] → Gilmour Space
```

**Your Role:** Tier 3 (aerospace component manufacturing)
- You are the **hub** connecting upstream suppliers (Lynas, Iluka, Pilbara) to downstream launch providers
- You hold AS 9100 certification (aerospace quality gold standard)
- You have multi-facility presence (VIC, NSW, QLD) providing supply chain resilience
- Without Magellan, none of these pathways achieve integration

---

## What We're Asking

Participate in formal verification that confirms:
1. **Operational capability:** Your facilities are AS 9100 certified and capable of space-grade manufacturing
2. **Supply chain participation:** You have relationships (or willingness to establish them) with Tier 1-2 suppliers (Lynas, Iluka, Pilbara) and Tier 4 launch providers (Equatorial Launch, Southern Launch)
3. **Standards compliance:** Your manufacturing processes meet aerospace quality standards

**You are not being asked to:**
- Disclose proprietary manufacturing processes
- Expose detailed cost/margin information
- Guarantee future procurement (this is verification, not a contract)
- Accept new regulatory burden

**You are being asked to:**
- Confirm publicly that Australian-sourced materials can flow through your manufacturing
- Allow ASA verification of your existing AS 9100 certification
- Participate in one joint stakeholder call with Lynas and Equatorial Launch Australia
- Commit to exploring formal ASA Statement of Engagement

## The Process

**ASA Statement of Engagement** reviews:
1. Magellan's AS 9100 certification (already yours)
2. Your facility capability for space-grade manufacturing
3. Your willingness to work with Australian suppliers (Lynas, Iluka, etc.)
4. Typical inter-tier relationship verification (do you actually buy from them, or just theoretically could?)

It is **not** a new quality system—it references your existing AS 9100 and operational record.

---

## One Key Question We're Asking

**Are the upstream/downstream relationships already established, or do they need to be built?**

This is critical for verification timeline:
- **If relationships exist:** Verification is straightforward (ASA confirms existing transactions)
- **If relationships are new:** Verification requires demonstrating feasibility (procurement trials, technical compatibility assessments)

We're calling to understand whether Magellan currently buys from Lynas/Iluka, or whether this would require establishing new relationships.

## Questions We Expect You'll Have

**Q: Is this confidential?**  
A: No. The SoE will name Magellan as the manufacturing hub for verified pathways. Magellan's role becomes public. However, specific details about relationships or processes can potentially be generalized (discussed with ASA).

**Q: What about competition sensitivity?**  
A: The verification focuses on capability and standards, not commercial relationships or cost. We can discuss with ASA how to verify capability while protecting commercial confidentiality.

**Q: What's the cost?**  
A: No direct cost to Magellan. ASA fees and coordination costs are covered by TerAustralis. Your time investment: ~20-30 hours over 3 months (documentation, internal coordination, potential facility audit).

**Q: Do we need board approval?**  
A: That's your decision. Our timeline allows for it—we're not asking for formal commitment until week 11-12, giving you time to consult internally after the week 10 exploratory conversation.

**Q: What if we currently don't buy from Lynas/Iluka?**  
A: That's the honest answer. The question is: could Magellan technically integrate these materials into your manufacturing? And would you be willing to trial procurement as part of verification? This would be explored in the week 10 conversation.

**Q: Will we have to change our manufacturing processes?**  
A: No. This verifies your existing AS 9100 processes. If inter-tier coordination requires technical trials (e.g., testing Australian REE in your existing processes), that's low-risk exploratory work, not process change.

**Q: Who else will be on the Small Council call?**  
A: Lynas Rare Earths (upstream supplier) and Equatorial Launch Australia (downstream integrator). This is the first time all three tiers will discuss coordination together. It's a working session, not a ceremonial call.
