## Lynas Conversation Scope

**Opening (2 min)**
- "We're exploring whether Australian industrial sovereignty in space can be formally verified via Australian Standards Association (ASA) Statement of Engagement. You're the critical minerals Tier 1-2 foundation. We want to understand your interest and any constraints."

**Context (3 min)**
- "We've documented three exemplar pathways: REE → avionics (Mount Weld + Lynas Perth processing → Magellan → Equatorial Launch). The other two follow the same pattern with Iluka titanium and Pilbara lithium."
- "All three tiers exist, are certified, and are operational. This isn't asking you to change anything."
- "ASA verification would mean: independent confirmation that Lynas' capabilities, standards compliance, and role in the supply chain are as we've documented. It's public. It's what makes Australian sovereignty *provable*."

**Conversation (35-40 min)**

1. **Appetite** (5-10 min)
   - "Do you see strategic value in being publicly recognized as the anchor of Australian aerospace independence?"
   - Listen for: enthusiastic yes / hesitant maybe / concerned no / need to understand more
   - If hesitant: "What would need to be true for this to be a 'yes'?"

2. **Participation Scope** (5-10 min)
   - "If we move forward, what would your participation look like from Lynas' perspective?"
   - Listen for: documentation review / executive interview / site visit / written statement of capability
   - Clarify: "You're not being asked to conduct special operations, change procurement, or guarantee future supply. We're verifying what you already do."

3. **Constraints** (5-10 min)
   - "Are there confidentiality constraints, supplier agreements, or regulatory considerations we should understand upfront?"
   - Listen for: customer confidentiality / procurement sensitivity / regulatory disclosure limits / internal approval workflows
   - Probe gently: "Would you need internal sign-off? From whom? What's the timeline?"

4. **Documentation** (5-10 min)
   - "What documentation can you point to that demonstrates Mount Weld extraction standards, Perth processing certifications, and compliance with aerospace supply protocols?"
   - Listen for: willingness to share / documentation gaps / need for redaction / third-party audit references
   - If hesitant: "Would an ASA auditor touring the facility suffice? Or do we need paper trails?"

5. **Timeline & Next Steps** (2-5 min)
   - "If we can work through [constraint from above], would you be willing to participate in a joint conversation with Magellan and Equatorial Launch Australia next week (Oct 14-25)?"
   - If yes: "That call will be 2-3 hours and cover how the three tiers coordinate. Would your [CEO/Strategy/Investor Relations] attend?"
   - If hesitant: "What would unblock this for you by next week?"

**Closing (1 min)**
- "Thank you for this. We'll follow up with [date/method] to confirm participation or discuss next steps."

## Magellan Conversation Scope

**Opening (2 min)**
- "You're the critical Tier 3 manufacturing hub in all three of our exemplar pathways. We're exploring whether ASA can formally verify that these pathways work end-to-end. We need to understand your confidence in the cross-tier relationships and your appetite for formal recognition."

**Context (3 min)**
- "Path 1: REE → avionics (Lynas → Magellan → ELA). Path 2: titanium → structure (Iluka → Magellan → Southern Launch). Path 3: lithium → power (Pilbara → Magellan → Gilmour Space)."
- "You're the visible connector in all three. That's powerful for both sides: you're recognized as Australia's aerospace manufacturing anchor, and we prove the supply chain works."
- "ASA verification doesn't require you to change anything. It's confirmation that what you already do—integrate Tier 1-2 materials into space-grade components—is standards-compliant and works."

**Conversation (35-40 min)**

1. **Cross-Tier Confidence** (10-15 min)
   - "How confident are you in the cross-tier relationships we've documented? Lynas → Magellan → launch providers?"
   - Listen for: very confident / mostly confident / needs validation / surprised by some connections
   - If surprised: "Which relationships need exploration? Should we include them in the joint conversation?"
   - Probe: "Do you have active MOUs, contracts, or certifications with these suppliers?"

2. **Documentation & Verification** (5-10 min)
   - "What documentation can you share that demonstrates these cross-tier relationships, your aerospace certifications, and standards compliance?"
   - Listen for: supply agreements / procurement records / quality certifications / site documentation / customer references
   - Clarify: "Would an ASA auditor need to visit your facilities? Interview your procurement team? Review supplier files?"

3. **Strategic Alignment** (5-10 min)
   - "How does being publicly positioned as Australia's aerospace manufacturing anchor align with Magellan's business strategy?"
   - Listen for: investor narrative / competitive advantage / recruitment tool / supply chain security story
   - If hesitant: "What would make this strategically valuable for Magellan?"

4. **Constraints & Approvals** (5-10 min)
   - "Are there confidentiality constraints with suppliers, customers, or regulators that would limit what we can verify publicly?"
   - Listen for: customer NDA concerns / supplier sensitivity / regulatory disclosure limits / internal approval needed
   - Probe: "Would anonymizing supplier names solve the issue? Can you check with [internal stakeholder]?"

5. **Participation & Timeline** (2-5 min)
   - "Would you be willing to participate in a joint conversation with Lynas and Equatorial Launch next week (Oct 14-25) where we align on how to prove these relationships exist and are standards-compliant?"
   - If yes: "Who from Magellan should attend? VP Operations? Chief Engineer? Someone with procurement visibility?"
   - If hesitant: "What would unblock this?"

**Closing (1 min)**
- "Thank you for walking through this. We'll confirm participation by [date] and send you the joint meeting agenda."

## ELA Conversation Scope

**Opening (2 min)**
- "You're the endpoint that makes this real. If Lynas and Magellan commit, and you confirm launch integration capability, that's how we prove Australian sovereignty works. We want to explore your readiness and constraints."

**Context (3 min)**
- "We've documented that Australian-sourced critical minerals can be processed, manufactured into aerospace components, and launched from Australian soil. But it only works if all three tiers—extract/process, manufacture, integrate/launch—say 'yes.' You're the 'yes' that completes the chain."
- "ASA verification means: independent confirmation that ELA can integrate and launch Australian-manufactured components. It's not asking you to conduct a test launch or modify facilities. It's verification of existing readiness."

**Conversation (35-40 min)**

1. **Launch Integration Capability** (5-10 min)
   - "Can ELA integrate and launch Australian-manufactured aerospace components?"
   - Listen for: yes / mostly yes / depends on components / need to explore
   - Probe: "What types of payloads / components have you integrated before? What's your confidence in Australian-sourced materials?"

2. **Launch Licensing & Operational Readiness** (10-15 min)
   - "What documentation demonstrates ELA's launch licensing and operational readiness to verify?"
   - Listen for: CASA licensing / operational permits / safety certifications / launch manifest history / customer references
   - Clarify: "Would ASA need to review licensing docs? Conduct a facility tour? Interview your operations team?"
   - Probe: "What would a verification audit look like from ELA's perspective?"

3. **Customer Confidentiality & Constraints** (5-10 min)
   - "Are there customer confidentiality or customer approval requirements we should understand?"
   - Listen for: commercial sensitivity / customer approval workflows / NDA concerns / disclosure limitations
   - If blocked: "Would anonymizing launch data solve this? Can we verify capability without revealing customers?"

4. **Strategic Positioning** (5-10 min)
   - "How does this align with ELA's business strategy and positioning?"
   - Listen for: market differentiation / investor narrative / supply chain security story / government relations
   - If hesitant: "What would make formal verification valuable for ELA?"

5. **Participation & Timeline** (2-5 min)
   - "Would you be willing to participate in a joint conversation with Lynas and Magellan next week (Oct 14-25) where we align on the full supply chain verification?"
   - If yes: "Who from ELA should attend? CTO? Program Lead? Someone with launch operations visibility?"
   - If hesitant: "What needs to happen for this to be a 'yes'?"

**Closing (1 min)**
- "Thank you for this. We'll confirm the joint meeting timing and send the agenda by [date]."
