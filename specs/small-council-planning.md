## Strategic Intent

The three lead suppliers (Lynas, Magellan, Equatorial Launch) have already publicly demonstrated the capabilities documented in the Onshore Chain One-Pager. The vision is validated. Small Council's purpose is to **negotiate formal participation in Australian Standards Association (ASA) Statement of Engagement verification**.

**Success metric:** By end of 90-day period (Nov 28, 2026), at least one ASA SoE engagement letter sent with commitment from lead suppliers OR clear path to engagement finalized.

## Conversation Roadmap

### Phase 1: Pre-engagement (Week 7-9)

**Objective:** Prepare the negotiation. Do NOT approach suppliers yet.

**Activities:**
1. **ASA Pre-engagement** — Contact ASA Standards Officer directly (not via suppliers)
   - Question: "Is Statement of Engagement the right mechanism for this verification scope?"
   - Clarify: What are SoE requirements, timeline, cost, process?
   - Output: ASA guidance document that strengthens the supplier conversation

2. **Prepare one-page briefing** — Executive summary for each supplier
   - What: Three exemplar pathways they're part of
   - Why: Australian industrial sovereignty, standards verification
   - How: ASA SoE process, timeline, commitment level expected
   - Next: "Would your team be interested in exploring formal verification?"

3. **Identify specific contacts** — Get decision-maker names and contact paths for each org
   - Lynas: Investor relations can route to CEO/strategy
   - Magellan: Operations VP or Chief Engineer
   - ELA: CTO or program lead

### Phase 2: Initial Approach (Week 10, early)

**Objective:** Schedule individual conversations. Keep small council concept separate for now.

**Activities:**
1. **Individual outreach to each supplier (3 separate conversations)**
   - Medium: Phone call or in-person meeting preferred (not email)
   - Duration: 30-45 minutes
   - Attendees: Supplier decision-maker + 1 aide; TerAustralis + witness
   - Goal: Explore interest, understand constraints, identify next steps
   
2. **Each conversation follows this arc:**
   - **Opening:** "We've documented a complete Australian supply chain for space-capable components. You're a critical node. Want to talk about formal verification?"
   - **The One-Pager:** Show them the pathway they're in (REE, titanium, or launch integration)
   - **ASA Process:** Explain SoE mechanism (pre-vetted with ASA)
   - **The Ask:** "Would your organization be interested in formal ASA verification that your components meet space standards? Timeline roughly Q4 2026."
   - **Listen:** Constraints, appetite, timeline, who else needs to approve
   - **Close:** "We'll synthesize what we hear and come back with a concrete proposal."

### Phase 3: Small Council Call (Week 10-11)

**Objective:** Bring all three together to discuss collective participation.

**Format:**
- **Duration:** 2-3 hours
- **Location:** Neutral, virtual (Zoom/Teams), or in-person if all three can travel (unlikely)
- **Attendees:** Same three suppliers + TerAustralis core + witness
- **Structure:**
  1. **Opening** (15 min) — Why we brought these three companies together
  2. **Mutual context** (20 min) — Each supplier hears from the other two (builds collective confidence)
  3. **ASA verification roadmap** (30 min) — Concrete SoE timeline, scope, commitments
  4. **Coordination discussion** (45 min) — How do we verify cross-tier transactions? (Lynas→Magellan, Magellan→ELA, etc.)
  5. **Next steps** (30 min) — Who commits? To what? By when?
  6. **Break / follow-ups** (as needed)

**Key conversation points:**
- "This is a first-of-kind verification for Australian industrial sovereignty. You'll be the exemplars."
- "The roadmap is public. Your participation will be acknowledged publicly."
- "We're not asking you to change operations—we're asking you to formally verify what you already do."
- "ASA will oversee the verification, not us. This is independent confirmation."

### Phase 4: Negotiation & Commitment (Week 11-12)

**Objective:** Secure concrete commitments toward ASA SoE lodgement.

**Activities:**
1. **Follow-up with each supplier after council call**
   - Clarify any open questions
   - Secure written indication of interest (email confirmation, LOI, or statement of intent)

2. **Prepare ASA SoE proposal**
   - Document which suppliers are committing
   - Outline pathways to be verified
   - Propose timeline for SoE execution (typically 4-8 weeks after lodgement)

3. **ASA formal engagement letter**
   - Target: Sent by end of week 12 (Nov 28)
   - Scope: Three pathways, three lead suppliers, verification timeline

These are *suggested* talking points, warrant-labelled for the negotiation:

## Risk Mitigation

**Risk:** "Suppliers not interested in formal verification"  
**Mitigation:** Pre-conversation with ASA clarifies *why* verification is valuable (regulatory, market access, insurance). Offer to let ASA explain directly.

**Risk:** "Timing conflicts / scheduling challenge"  
**Mitigation:** Offer both virtual and in-person options. Split council into two sessions if needed (Lynas+Magellan in session 1; Equatorial Launch + negotiation in session 2).

**Risk:** "Suppliers ask for confidentiality / don't want public acknowledgment"  
**Mitigation:** Build this into the conversation: "This verification is public—your name and participation will be documented. Is that acceptable?" If not, reframe: "Would you participate if we anonymized you as 'Tier 1 REE processor'?" (Preferred approach: push for full attribution per Sovereign Lattice covenant.)

**Risk:** "ASA says 'not interested' or process is different than expected"  
**Mitigation:** Pivot to alternative verification frameworks (third-party audit, internal certification, industry association endorsement). Adjust roadmap accordingly but maintain commitment to verification.
