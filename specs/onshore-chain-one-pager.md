## Executive Summary

Australia possesses a complete sovereign industrial chain for space-capable components: from critical mineral extraction through advanced processing, aerospace-grade manufacturing, and launch integration. This document demonstrates three exemplar pathways, identifies lead Australian suppliers, and outlines the ASA engagement process for standards-aligned verification.

**Key Claim:** An Australian satellite or launch vehicle component can be fully sourced, processed, manufactured, and integrated within Australian facilities using domestically mined critical minerals and ASA-verified manufacturing processes.

## ASA Statement of Engagement (SoE)

### What is ASA SoE?

The Australian Standards Association (ASA) is Australia's peak non-governmental standards body. An **SoE (Statement of Engagement)** is a formal declaration that:
1. An organisation is adopting, developing, or implementing Australian Standards
2. The organisation's technical practices are verifiable against those standards
3. Independent audit and third-party verification have been conducted

### Process for TerAustralis Sovereignty Claim

1. **Notify ASA** of intention to lodge SoE for integrated Australian critical minerals → aerospace component chain
2. **Identify applicable standards:** 
   - AS 9100: Aerospace quality management
   - AS/NZS ISO 17025: Testing and calibration laboratory accreditation
   - AS 1418: Cranes (for launch facility operations)
   - Relevant materials and process standards (ISO 9001, AS EN standards)
3. **Third-party audit:** Commission independent audit firm to verify manufacturing, quality, and supply chain compliance
4. **Submit SoE to ASA Registry:** Public listing of verified capability
5. **Publish verification:** SoE becomes public record; verifiable by external parties

**Outcome:** An Australian government or private entity can demonstrably claim "100% Australian sourced and manufactured space component" with formal ASA-backed verification.

### Lodgement Reference

ASA lodgement process: https://www.standards.org.au/ (Statement of Engagement registry)
